# Lofi Production Bible (synthesis of 5 research reports)

Report tags: [H]=harmony, [R]=rhythm, [SD]=sound-design, [M]=mixing, [G]=generative. Ratified rule applied throughout: **where literature and our measurements (the reference corpus's spectrum in `examples/lofi_measure/corpus.rs`, the archive.org corpus's form in R11) disagree, the measurement wins.**

---

## 1. RULES — note-table generator (harmony / melody / form)

Each rule → the parameter it maps to here.

- **R1. Every chord carries a 7th, against the tracks.** Lofi Girl's 108 tracks read triads 59%, maj7 20, min7 16, dom7 3 (lv-chordia, a chord a beat, and 97% of a take of all 7ths reads as 7ths, so it isn't defaulting to triads), a chord's share of triads a property of its degree: I .78, ii .37, iii .45, IV .42, V .88, vi .59. Judged, those triads pull our takes away from the same tracks, in proportion: mean dFAD -.004 with none, +.006 at half those shares, +.024 at them. A change to the pad's or the keys' timbre re-tests this. → `compose::Degree`.
- **R3. Short diatonic loops, a chord a bar.** MEASURED: major roots I 34, IV 29, vi 12, V 10, ii 8, iii 4; minor i 35, ♭VI 27, iv 15, ♭VII 9, v 7, ♭III 5; 2.5% of bars on a root outside the key; beats a chord P25/P50/P75 3.1/4.0/5.1; IV↔I 28% of major's root changes, i↔♭VI 33% of minor's; a loop read every 2, 4, 8, 16 bars in 11, 44, 30, 9 tracks, 8 under-read (the measure reads 10 of 16 of our 8-bar takes as 4). → `MAJOR_LOOPS`, `MINOR_LOOPS`: rows hold those shares, 2, 4 and 8 bars long; `GEN_LOOP_BARS` leaves 16 unwritten.
- **R5. All twelve keys, 61% major**: no tonic holds more than 14 of the 108 tracks. → `harmony`'s key draw; `HarmonyStyle::major_share`, day .8 and night .42 averaging that.
- **R7. Register map (MIDI)**: bass root **31–42**, an octave lower where a chord voice under 48 sits within a P5 of it, the window that puts the mix's <62/<125 Hz power inside the archive.org corpus's IQR, the reference then; shell 3rd+7th **48–60**, spread ≥P4; extensions **60–72**; melody **72–84** (one octave); every two voices of a chord keep the low interval limits, the chart's lowest note for each interval's lower one (`compose`'s `LOW_INTERVAL_LIMITS`), which `Degree::voiced`'s floor keeps. Drop the 5th when >4 voices. → note-table register clamps. [H, robin-hoffmann.com/dfsb/low-interval-limits/, pianogroove.com shell voicings]
- **R8. Root position, every root in one octave**: the literature's nearest-voicing voice-leading, judged as closed inversions by least total semitones moved, took the panel from 59/36 to 42/53 and mean dFAD from -.004 to +.003. → `Degree::voiced`.
- **R9. Melody pool = pentatonic** (+ maj7 color tone on major; dorian 6 on minor); chord tones (3rd/7th preferred over root) on strong beats, extensions on weak. → melody note table, chord-relative. [H]
- **R10. Melody form**: 2-bar phrase = A + A′ (exactly one note or rhythm cell mutated); 4-bar period = call (ends off-tonic) + response (ends on chord tone); density 2–4 onsets/bar. → phrase generator. [H]
- **R11. A song has a form: one harmonic loop, an intro, a body whose lanes thin and return over it, an outro.** MEASURED on the archive.org corpus, the reference then (bar-synchronous self-similarity, controlled on the engine's songs and on edited tracks, 2026-10-25): an intro in 95% (P50 21 s, 8 bars long in 26 tracks and 4 in 14: sparse 72 — drums+bass out 27, drums 13, bass 10, drums+bass+keys 7, keys 5, unsplit 10, "keys" being the harmonic 150 Hz–2 kHz, which holds a lead too — fade-in 13, filtered 10, cold 5), an outro in 96% (P25/P50/P75 7/16/28 s: sparse 48 — drums+bass out 14, bass 11, drums 10, all but noise 8, unsplit 5 — fade 31, ring-out 10, filtered 7, cold 4); 91% of section boundaries keep the loop and 5 of 100 tracks have a second one; a drum drop-out in 60% (P50/P75/P90 1/2/3 a track, 2 or more in 27% of tracks and 3 or more in 12%; 1, 2 and 3 bars long for 40, 13 and 15% of them, the rest 4 or more, a quarter of tracks holding a 4–8-bar break; one-bar ones ending an 8-bar phrase 32% of the time; the first at P25/P50/P75 0.38/0.45/0.56 of the track; the bass leaving with 52% of them), a bass drop-out in 74% (59% of them keeping the beat), a mid-track whole-mix low-pass in 20%, mostly one bar; body loudness flat within 2.2 dB, so changes subtract. Lofi Girl's 150-track playlist agrees: five cores, sparse–dense–thinner–densest–sparse, on one loop (114 of 150), 72% start beatless, fade-outs 40%, P50 147 s [Fields-Meyer 2025, escholarship.org/uc/item/9w27s169]. → `compose::form`'s grammar; intensity layers on top as FMOD combines two automations of one lane: gains multiply, a mute wins, a cutoff takes the lower [fmod.com/docs/2.03/studio/advanced-topics.html#automation-curve-combination]; a song that starts with no beat keeps its intro's and outro's layers, drops their fades, filters and the dip, and plays its body whole (`Form::beatless`).
- **R12. Whole chords land 10–30 ms behind the grid** (composes with swing offsets). → per-stem lag offset. [H]
- **R13. Melody rhythm duration prior: 65% 8ths / 25% 16ths / 5% quarters / 5% halves.** → duration table. [G, mtsandra.github.io/blog/2023/lofi-generator/]

## 2. GROOVE numbers

- **Swing formula**: delay every even 16th by `(s − 0.5) × 8th-duration`. At 75 BPM (8th = 400 ms): 54%→+16 ms, 58%→+32 ms, 62%→+48 ms. [R, Roger Linn via brettworks.com]
- **Differential swing** (the Dilla move): hats **56–62%**, kick/snare **52–54%**. Single-knob fallback: 54–58%. [R, musicproductionwiki.com; SD gearspace Dilla thread agrees at 53–57%]
- **Lag bias** (element friction, not randomness): hats stay quantized; kick/snare dragged **+10–30 ms** late; alternative cheap version = whole-track delays, clap +20 ms / hats +35 ms. [R, grokipedia.com/page/Dilla_Time; splice.com/blog/lo-fi-beat-origin-sound/]
- **Jitter**: ±5–15 ms uniform per hit; snare biased late (never early). [R, lockah.net]
- **Velocity (0–127)**: kick 115–127, jitter ±0–5%, inter-bar wobble 15–20 units; snare backbeat 100–115; hats accents 110–127 / off-8ths 70–90 / fill-16ths 40–60, jitter ±10–15%; ghosts 25–50% (≈32–64; [R] says 38–64, [M] says 20–40 — use 32–64 for snare ghosts, 20–40 for the quietest kick ghosts). [R beatkitchen.io; M]
- **Patterns (16-step)**: MEASURED on Lofi Girl's stems, 59 of 108 tracks keep a backbeat on 2 and 4 and 22 a half-time snare (the rest too faint to tell), and a quarter put their off-eighths at about 62% of the pair while most sit almost straight. Boom-bap kick steps 1,7,11 + ghost 16th before snare; snare 5,13; hats straight 8ths. Sleepy/half-time: kick step 1, snare step 9 (rimshot/brush), quarter-note hats vel ≤70. [R, blog.native-instruments.com/what-is-boom-bap/; transmissionsamples.com]
- **Onset budget: 1.2–2/s total (MEASURED — prefer)**. Full boom-bap computes to 2.5–3/s → hats mixed low and few ghosts, or half-time. [R derivation vs our fingerprint]
- **BPM: 68–90 (MEASURED — prefer)**, center 74–80 (literature sweet spot sits inside our band). [R midimighty.com; M]
- **Sidechain**: MEASURED on Lofi Girl's stems, the bass and everything else dip about 6 dB under a kick and are back in 150–200 ms, a dip our own takes through the same separation don't show, so lofi plays it (`synth::SIDECHAIN_DB`) on every lane but the drums and the room, and on the plate's return, whose tail would fill the dip back in. In the literature, two regimes: transparent duck = attack 0–2 ms, release 50–100 ms, 4:1, **2–4 dB GR**; audible sway (sleepy states) = same attack, tempo-synced release ≈1/4 note (200–400 ms audible recovery), **3–6 dB GR**, never >6–8. [R, edmprod.com/sidechain-compression/; sonarworks.com]

## 3. SOUND targets per voice

**Pad** — extensions/upper-shell register (MIDI 60–72+). Slow attack / long release AR; dual-voice detune **±5–10 cents**. Brickwall LPF ~2–4 kHz; HPF ~140 Hz. Primary sidechain-pump target. Loop length incommensurate with all other stems (see §4) (declined here: the musical lanes phase-lock — `generated_beds_are_finite_phase_locked_and_in_the_sound_world`). [M fractal forum detune; SD modeaudio.com]

**Bass** — sine + tanh saturation for audible harmonics (the sub carries power, harmonics carry audibility — matches our <62 Hz fingerprint). Root-only below MIDI 48; line restricted to root/5th/leading-tone. Dead center, mono. Soft attack; ducked by kick (kick wins the transient, sub swells behind). Notes stay long: Lofi Girl's bass transcribes to 450 ms a note, but a bass cut to that length sat further from its stems' embedding (per-song FAD 0.57, against 0.43 long and 0.38 for its held-out tracks), and the judges heard the holes it left under an empty room. [SD edmprod.com/lofi-hip-hop/; G devpost lofigen; M joeysturgistones.com]

**Keys (EP pluck)** — the genre's core voice. Steal the Rhodes physics: **displacement/velocity-dependent asymmetric waveshaping that raises the 2nd harmonic** as velocity rises = "bell vs bark" (conforg.fr/isma2014 paper); very short attack transient. Register 48–72. HPF 60–100 Hz, brickwall LPF ~2 kHz; **notch 200–700 Hz if low notes ring** (the documented procedural-lofi failure). Chords 10–30 ms late. Optional: 180° stereo-opposed sine tremolo at 1/4–1/2-note rate; felt variant = quieter attack partials + per-note filtered-noise thump layer. [SD; G devpost; SD spectrasonics Keyscape manual]

**Sparkle (melody)** — MIDI 72–84, pentatonic, 2–4 onsets/bar. Fast attack / medium decay AR. **Bitcrush this layer (8–12 bit)** — the top layer is where the crunch reads. Any reverb return HPF ≥300 Hz. [G Sonic Pi kalimba-through-bitcrusher recipe, in-thread.sonic-pi.net; SD unison.audio; voxbooster.com]

**Drums** — 12-bit / ~26 kHz sample-rate-reduction reference (SP-1200, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-mu_SP-1200); saturate **then** LPF 3–6 kHz; no bus HPF: MEASURED on Lofi Girl's stems (P50 of 108 tracks, 2026-11-01), a kick's power sits at 50 Hz and falls 20 dB in 115 ms, a snare's around 1.1 kHz in 50 ms, and the drums hold 30% of a mix's power (IQR 22–40%), the bass 26%, the rest 38%; **through the tape chain, not around it** (sat 25–30%, wow/flutter ~15%). Kick = sub boom + mid punch; **lengthening the kick attack envelope is a groove parameter** ("slurped"). Snare tight/clap-like, distorted then filtered; brush/rim for sleepy states, −6 dB further back. [R native-instruments, attackmagazine.com, unison.audio]

**Texture (crackle/hiss/foley)** — crackle bed **−24 to −18 dB below master, HPF ~1 kHz** (declined here, MEASURED 2026-10-27: the bed 3 dB up toward this range, `synth`'s `TEXTURE_PEAK` and the stream's texture staging together, lost every judge on 95 takes — Audiobox CE 0/95, TuneJury 0/95, FAD 5/90) (hiss peaks 4–8 kHz, must sit *on top*, not as broadband mud) (declined here, MEASURED 2026-10-26: a hiss band-passed 1–8 kHz, about −21 dB under the mix, lost every judge on 95 takes — Audiobox CE 4/91, MuQ-Eval 12/83, FAD 0/95 — so the hiss stays under `synth`'s `HISS_HZ` low-pass, about −25 dB under). Per-click spectral variation (constant-timbre clicks read as static — arxiv.org/pdf/2206.06259); density = a "record age" param, a few clicks/s (declined here, MEASURED 2026-10-26: 2 pops/s lost to `synth`'s `CRACKLE_POPS_PER_SEC` on 95 takes — Audiobox CE 37/58, MuQ-Eval 0/95, FAD 35/60). **Sidechain-duck the crackle keyed off kick+snare**; automate it up in sparse sections. Foley (rain/café) sparse and section-signaling only; the one published ratio: rain ≈ −2.5 dB, café ≈ −7.6 dB vs music (sleep-video levels — in-track sit well below). Crackle doubles as room tone masking loop seams. [M selektaudio.com; SD beatproduction.net, songnara.com]

**Tape chain (shared)** — wow **0.5–2 Hz** (Goodhertz default 0.55 Hz/15%), flutter **6–20 Hz**, randomized "flux" rate/depth + wow→flutter cross-modulation (the difference between vibrato and tape), **stereo-opposed per channel** for width; audible warble = depth >~0.1–0.2%, broken-walkman ≈0.5–2%. Portable physics if wanted: Chowdhury DAFx-19 loss FIR + Jiles-Atherton constants (ccrma.stanford.edu/~jatin/420/tape/TapeModel_DAFx.pdf, GPL impl — port the math, not the code). [SD; M goodhertz manual]

## 4. MIX targets (measurement-reconciled)

- **Band shares, centroid and LTAS slope: 108 tracks of Lofi Girl's own playlist (MEASURED), `examples/lofi_measure/corpus.rs`'s `CORPUS`, which `lofi_measure` reads every take against.** The archive.org corpus before it read 445 Hz where these read 211 (owner, 2026-11-01: "挺不lofi的 lofi girl的曲目你没采用？"); the two Lofi Girl masters it had displaced were nearer: 55% under 125 Hz here.
- **Enforce by subtraction, not boost**: HPF every non-bass stem ~140 Hz; HPF master at 30 Hz; sub register belongs to the bass stem alone (R7). [M lofiweekly.com]
- **Head-bump conflict — RESOLVED (2026-08)**: the chain bumped 80–120 Hz while literature pins 15 ips ≈ 60 Hz / 30 ips ≈ 120 Hz, and the bass lane's fundamentals (36–73 Hz) landed under the old floor. `lofi_post` now bumps the widened **60–120 Hz**; the frozen night-pad 62–125 pin re-measured 0.191 → 0.299 and the batch was re-LISTEN-gated. [SD help.uaudio.com Oxide manual; M]
- **6.5 kHz rolloff: keep.** Real cassette rolls off 10–12 kHz — ours is deliberately darker, matching genre practice and anti-fatigue (2–5 kHz is the ear's fatigue band). MEASURED 2026-10-26 on 95 judge takes: a +3 dB shelf above 1 kHz lost every judge (Audiobox CE 0/95, MuQ-Eval 0/95, FAD 5/90), and a −4 dB cut at 350 Hz won FAD 95/0 but lost the other two 0/95. [SD ultraferric.com; M babyaud.io]
- **Mud guard**: the most common defect is excess 250–500 Hz, not missing bass; keys notch 200–700 Hz on ringing. Confine tanh saturation to 100 Hz–10 kHz (low-end intermodulation guard). [M trackscore.ai; G; M masteringthemix.com]
- **Bus order & glue**: corrective EQ → **glue comp (1.5–2:1, attack 10–30 ms, release ~150 ms, ≤2 dB GR, total chain <4 dB)** (declined here, MEASURED 2026-10-27: a glue comp at 2:1, 20 ms attack, 150 ms release and a 6 dB knee, reducing about 1 dB and 2 dB at its 99th percentile, lost the panel on 95 takes — Audiobox CE 1/94, TuneJury 17/78 — though FAD favoured it 87/8) → tape chain (sat/wow/flutter) → limiter. [M audiospectra.net]
- **Loudness**: **−16 to −14 LUFS integrated, ≤−1 dBTP**, no heavy limiting — an office bed plays under speech and quieter/more dynamic than a release master; preserved dynamics are themselves anti-fatigue. [M veniamastering.studio, widenisland.com]
- **Stereo**: sub + kick dead center; **side-channel HPF ~150 Hz @ 6 dB/oct** (vinyl elliptical EQ — a slope, not a brickwall); width comes from the top: stereo-opposed wow/flutter, 180° EP autopan, slightly-panned duplicate noise beds. [SD gearspace mastering thread, flotownmastering.com]
- **Anti-repetition**: stem loop lengths mutually **incommensurate** (Eno's 23½ s / 25⅞ s / 29 15/16 s model — never re-align) (declined here: the musical lanes phase-lock — `generated_beds_are_finite_phase_locked_and_in_the_sound_world`); texture layers get ±2% pitch / ±3 dB per-instance variation; QA gate = **listen ≥4 repetitions** (the brain catches the seam on rep 3–4). [M reverbmachine.com, soundcy.com, audioedit.io]

## 5. GENERATIVE lessons (prior art)

- **The #1 repeated failure: naive Markov/stochastic harmony is directionless and never resolves.** Two independent authors hit it and abandoned it; fixed **jazz-turnaround grammar + substitution operators** (secondary dominants; base strings I–vi–ii–V, ii–V–I–vi) won. If stochastic flavor is ever wanted: forward-chain + backward-chain-from-resolution joined at a pivot. §1's R3 keeps the lesson, a fixed grammar, with Lofi Girl's loops as the grammar. [G devpost.com/software/lofi-generator; medium.com/@w.patrick.kelly]
- **Chord-relative melody encoding** (jacbz/Lofi, Apache-2.0): melody as scale degrees 0–15 against the *current chord*, 0=rest — quantizing to the chord's scale is the trick that keeps generated melodies consonant. Adopt the schema (Roman-numeral chord ints 0–8, valence/energy conditioning scalars map to busy-ness). [G github.com/jacbz/Lofi]
- **Constrain the form; don't skip it**: best prior art tops out at 32-bar AABA with per-section stem add/drop; Magenta chose lofi *because* constrained structure makes "always sounds acceptable" achievable. Real tracks have a form (R11), so the form is drawn from a small measured grammar. [G magenta.withgoogle.com/lofi-player]
- **Static FX beat automated FX**: the Devpost author abandoned algorithmic effect automation as sounding worse than fixed settings — keep the tape chain parameters static per track, vary per seed. [G]
- **The hard bugs are mix gremlins, not note choice** (low-piano ringing → the 200–700 Hz notch; missing transduction noise). Musicology confirms rolled-off highs + hiss/crackle ARE the genre's authenticity signal — our tape chain + crackle bed is the load-bearing layer, not garnish. [G Winston & Saywood 2019, IASPM; Neal, Organised Sound]
